title: Email newsletters
description: Subscribe to creators, communities and tags by email on Ecency, how automatic digests work, and how Ecency Pro creators send their own posts to subscribers.
---

Readers can follow creators, communities, tags or all of Ecency by email. Ecency sends a digest of new posts on a weekly or monthly schedule.

## For readers

- **Subscribe:** click **Email digest** on a creator's profile or a community, or **Get #tag by email** on a tag feed. Enter your email, pick how often (**Weekly** or **Monthly**) and click **Subscribe**. You don't need a Hive account.
- **Confirm:** Ecency emails you a confirmation link. Until you click it, the button shows **Digest: confirm email**.
- **Manage:** logged-in users see every digest under **Email digests** in [Settings](/ecency/settings/), where you can switch between weekly and monthly, or leave.
- **Unsubscribe:** every email has a one-click unsubscribe link and a link to stop all Ecency emails.

You get at most one email per subscription for each period.

## Automatic digests

Subscribers to a creator get a digest of that creator's posts from the period. Weekly periods run Monday to Monday (UTC) and monthly ones follow the calendar month. Each issue goes out at 09:00 UTC on the day the period ends, and delivery is spread over the next few hours.

A tag digest needs at least 5 different authors posting in that tag in the last 30 days. Otherwise you'll see a message that the tag is too quiet.

## For creators with Ecency Pro

[Ecency Pro](/ecency/ecency-pro/) creators can send their own posts to their subscribers instead of waiting for the automatic digest:

- **One post:** open your own post and use **Send to email subscribers**. It works on your own top-level posts that have a title and aren't NSFW or hidden.
- **A digest you put together:** use **Compose email digest**, pick 2 to 10 of your posts, add a subject (up to 120 characters) and an intro (up to 500 characters).

Ecency shows your subscriber count and a **Copy subscribe link** button you can share to grow your list. A send replaces that period's automatic digest, so subscribers still get only one email. You can send once per period for each schedule (weekly and monthly are separate).

Lists that get too many spam complaints or bounced emails are suspended automatically to protect delivery for everyone, until the Ecency team reviews them.

Community owners and admins can send to their community's subscribers. Moderators can see the list but not send.
