Ecency has four ways to give an account or a post a lift. **Boost+**, **RC top-up** and **Promote** are paid with Ecency Points. **Account Boost** is an in-app purchase in the Ecency mobile app. You can get Points by [buying them](/ecency/buy-points/) or by [earning them](/ecency/daily-rewards/).

<Screenshot src={shotPerks} alt="The Ecency perks page with Get more points, Promote your post and other perk cards" caption="All the perks start from ecency.com/perks." />

## Boost+

Boost+ swaps your Points for a temporary Hive Power delegation to an account. More Hive Power means more Resource Credits and more voting influence while the boost lasts.

### How to use Boost+

1. Open **Perks** on Ecency and choose **Get more Resources (Boost+)**.
2. Enter the **account** to boost. It can be your own account or someone else's.
3. Pick a **duration** in days. The price for each duration is shown next to it.
4. Confirm. Your request activates in a couple of minutes.

An account that is already boosted can't be boosted again until the current boost ends.

## Account Boost

Account Boost is an **in-app purchase** in the Ecency mobile app. It gives an account a **30-day Hive Power delegation** and doesn't spend Points. You can boost **any account**, not just your own.

### How to use Account Boost

- **Mobile:** open **Perks** and choose **Account boost**, pick the account and complete the purchase.
- **Web:** on **Perks**, choose **Out of Resource credits? Get more using Ecency mobile app**. Select the account and scan the QR code with the Ecency mobile app to finish the purchase there.

Ecency can revoke a boost if the stake is used improperly.

## RC top-up

An RC top-up spends Points on a short-term, RC-only delegation so you can keep posting when your Resource Credits run low. Ecency offers it when you try to publish with low RC (look for **Top up RC**). Pick a duration and confirm. The top-up appears shortly. You can only have one active top-up at a time.

## Promote

Promote puts a post in front of more readers. Promoted posts appear in the **Promoted** feed and in other places across Ecency on the web and in the mobile app.

### How to use Promote

1. Open the post's **three-dot menu** and choose **Promote**, or go to **Perks** and choose **Promote your post**.
2. Pick the post and a **duration**. The available durations and their prices are shown in the app.
3. Confirm. The post joins the promoted list.

You can promote your own posts or anyone else's. Ecency can remove posts from the promoted list if they break common internet etiquette.

## Comparison

| | Boost+ | Account Boost | RC top-up | Promote |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| What you get | Temporary HP delegation | 30-day HP delegation | Short-term RC delegation | More visibility for one post |
| Paid with | Points | In-app purchase | Points | Points |
| Helps with | RC and voting influence | RC and voting influence | RC only | Readers and engagement |
| For | Any account | Any account | Your account | Any post |
| Where | Perks (web and mobile) | Mobile app (QR from web Perks) | When RC is low | Post menu, Perks |

## Which one should I choose?

- **I'm out of RC and just want to keep posting:** use an **RC top-up**.
- **I want more RC and voting influence for a while:** use **Boost+** (Points) or **Account Boost** (in-app purchase).
- **I want more people to see a specific post:** use **Promote**.
- **I want to help a friend's account:** Boost+ and Account Boost both work for any account.

None of these guarantee votes or rewards. See [Resource Credits](/hive/resource-credits/) to learn how RC works.
