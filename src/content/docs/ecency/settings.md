title: Settings
description: Every Ecency setting on web and mobile, from currency and theme to notifications, chat privacy and email digests.
---

Ecency keeps your preferences in one place on each platform. On the web, open your profile and choose **Settings** (the address is `ecency.com/@yourname/settings`). The Settings tab only appears on your own profile while you are logged in. On mobile, open the side menu and tap **Settings**.

## Web settings

The web Settings page is made of cards.

### Profile

Edit your display name, about text, profile image, cover image, website and location. See [Profile](/ecency/profile/) for the details and limits.

### Preferences

- **Notification alerts**: turn all Ecency notifications **On** or **Off** in one switch.
- **Currency**: the currency used to show estimated values across the site.
- **Language**: the interface language.
- **Theme Settings**: **System Default**, **Day mode** or **Night mode**.
- **Show NSFW content**: **On** shows posts tagged NSFW, **Off** hides them.
- **Image server**: the server that loads images for you. Keep the default unless images fail to load on your network.

### Support Ecency

Two optional ways to support Ecency, both **Off** by default:

- **Post beneficiary**: adds @ecency as a beneficiary on your new posts at 1%, 5%, 10% or 25%. It is added automatically when you start a new post, and you can remove it from any single post.
- **Curation reward holdback**: if you delegate Hive Power to @ecency, Ecency pays you a daily share of curation rewards. Here you choose what percent (5%, 10%, 25%, 50% or 100%) of that payout Ecency keeps as support.

### Chat Privacy

Choose who can send you direct messages: **Everyone**, **Only people I follow**, or **No one (disable DMs)**. See [Chats](/ecency/chats/).

### Email digests

Lists every email digest you receive (your notification digest, communities and creators) with its status. You can switch each one between **Weekly** and **Monthly**, **Leave** a single digest, or **Stop all Ecency email** to your address. Stopping all email does not affect account emails such as password recovery.

### Keys & permissions

**Open permissions** takes you to your keys and app authorizations. See [Manage keys](/guides/manage-keys/).

### Referral link

Copy your referral link to invite friends. See [Referral program](/get-started/referral/).

### Per-type notification settings

On the web, open the notifications panel and use its menu. Under **Sound/Popup Settings** you can switch individual types on or off: votes, replies, mentions, favorites, bookmarks, tags, reblogs, follows, transfers, delegations, payouts, account updates, weekly earnings and scheduled posts.

## Mobile settings

The mobile **Settings** screen has these groups.

### General

- **Currency** and **Language**.
- **Chat Privacy** (same three options as on the web).
- **Server**: the Hive API node the app talks to.
- **Image Server**: where images load from.
- **NSFW**: **Always show**, **Always warn** or **Always hide**.
- **Appearance**: **Light**, **Dark** or **Device settings**.
- **Show Images**: turn images in posts on or off.
- **PIN code**: lock the app with a PIN. Once a PIN is set you can also enable **Finger Print / Face Unlock** and **Reset Pin Code**.

### Push notifications

Turn **Push Notification** on, then choose the types you want: follow, vote, comment, mention, favorites, bookmarks, tags, reblog, transfers, scheduled post published, delegations, payouts, account updates and weekly earnings. If your device does not support push, you only get notifications while the app is open.

### Support Ecency

The same two options as on the web: **Share post rewards with Ecency** and **Hold back part of curation rewards**, each with a percent.

### Other entries

- **Email digests**: tap **Manage** to subscribe, change cadence, leave a digest or stop all email.
- **Manage Authorities**: view and manage your keys. The app asks you to secure it with a PIN before it shows private keys.
- **Delete Account**: sends a deletion request for your Ecency data and logs you out. It cannot erase anything already written to the Hive blockchain.

## Muted users and bot comments

Muting people is not part of the Settings page. See [Mute users](/ecency/mute-users/) for how to mute and unmute someone.

In the mobile app, comments from known bot accounts are grouped into their own collapsed section under a post, so they do not crowd out human replies. There is no switch for this.
