The AI image generator turns a short text description into an image you can use in your posts. Each image is paid with [Ecency Points](/ecency/ecency-points/), and you need to be logged in.

## Where to find it

- **Web:** go to **Perks** and open **AI Image Generator**, or visit [ecency.com/perks/ai-generator](https://ecency.com/perks/ai-generator). You can also open it from the post editor toolbar with the **AI Image** button, which lets you insert the result straight into your post.
- **Mobile:** in the post editor, tap the image button with the small **AI** badge. You can also reach it from the Points screen.

## Create an image

1. Under **Describe the image you want to create**, write your prompt. For example: "A beautiful sunset over mountains with purple clouds". Prompts can be up to 1,000 characters. When you open the generator from the editor, it may offer a suggested prompt based on your post (**Use: ...**).
2. Pick an **Aspect ratio**: 1:1, 16:9, 9:16, 4:3, 3:4, 3:2 or 2:3.
3. Pick a **Quality boost** level: 1x, 2x, 4x or 6x. Higher levels use more capable image models and cost more.
4. Check the **Cost** against your **Balance**, then click **Generate Image**.

Generation can take a little while. If the image is ready but still finishing its upload, the generator fetches it for you without charging again.

## Cost

The price depends on the quality level. At the time of writing a 1x image costs 150 Points, and each level multiplies that:

| Quality boost | Cost |
| --- | --- |
| 1x | 150 Points |
| 2x | 300 Points |
| 4x | 600 Points |
| 6x | 900 Points |

All aspect ratios cost the same. The generator always shows the current cost before you generate.

[Ecency Pro](/ecency/ecency-pro/) members get **one free 1x image per day** (the day resets at midnight UTC). Higher quality levels are never free.

If generation fails, your Points are refunded. If your prompt is rejected by the content policy, the Points are not refunded, so keep prompts within the rules.

## Use your image

When the image is ready you can:

- **Insert into post:** adds the image to the post you are writing (when you opened the generator from the editor).
- **Download** the image.
- **Share** it (mobile).
- **Generate Again** with the same or a changed prompt.

On the web, each new image is also added to your Ecency image gallery, and the **History** tab lists your recent generations so you can insert or download them later.

## Disclose AI images

When you insert a generated image into a post, Ecency ticks **AI-generated media** in the post's AI usage disclosure for you. Readers then see a small **AI usage disclosed by author** badge. You can review this setting before publishing. See [AI writing assistant](/ecency/ai-assist/#ai-usage-disclosure) for details.
