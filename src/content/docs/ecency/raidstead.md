Raidstead is a browser game on Ecency: "Communities against the swarm". Your Hive community is your alliance. Every week a pest drifts into town. You scout its weakness, hit it together with your alliance, and build your town between raids.

Play at [ecency.com/raidstead](https://ecency.com/raidstead). You can also open it from **Raidstead** in the Ecency menu or from the Raidstead card on the Perks page.

## Sign in

Raidstead uses your Hive account:

- If you are logged in to Ecency, you are signed in automatically. Select **Play as @yourname**.
- If you are not logged in, select **Sign in with your wallet** and sign once with Hive Keychain, Hive Keeper or Peak Vault. After that you stay signed in on that device.

Your keys never go to the game. You can sign out from the menu with **Sign out of Raidstead**.

## Pick your alliance

Your alliance is one of the Hive [communities](/hive/communities/) you have joined on Ecency. Pick it in **Pick your alliance** at the start. It stays the same until the season ends. If you haven't joined any communities yet, use **Browse communities**, join one, and come back.

Everyone in a community plays on the same team, faces the same pest and builds the same town.

## How a week works

The **Field guide** in the menu explains the rules. In short:

1. **Scout the pest.** Each pest is weak to Ink, Signal or Forge. The right type does double damage. You get a free scout each day.
2. **Attack together.** Every attack costs 1 energy. Your heroes throw different types: the Scribe throws Ink, the Scout throws Signal and the Smith throws Forge. At half health a shield of gnats forms, so clear them first. At 75% a Clickbait Wasp may join and dodge you until you scout again.
3. **Build your town.** Wins bring materials. Spend them on buildings that help the next raid.

When the pest is chased off, everyone who raided that week shares the win.

### Energy

You get 5 energy a day, plus 1 energy for each Ecency [daily quest](/ecency/daily-rewards/) you finish (check in, publish a post, leave 3 comments). Once a day, the Herald can **Rally** for 5 more energy, which costs 50 Points. Some buildings and powers add more.

### The weekly pests

A season has four weekly pests, each with a trick:

- **Spam Beetle:** copies itself overnight if nobody attacks all day. Hit it at least once every day.
- **Doomscroll Slug:** heals overnight if nobody keeps it busy. Show up a little every day.
- **Echo Twins:** a hit only counts when the other twin is hit within 6 seconds. Alternate, and anyone in your alliance can finish the pair.
- **Copycat Queen** (season finale): changes her weakness 20 seconds after every scout. Scout, then hit hard with the right type.

## Your town

Buildings go through stages (Blueprint, Foundation, Half built, Finished) and cost materials. You must raid at least once in the season to help build. Examples:

- **Hall:** the town's level. No building can go higher than the Hall.
- **Watchtower:** one extra free scout a day.
- **Workshop:** crafts season powers such as Double Tap or Keen Eye. Crafting costs 2 shards.
- **Beacon:** +1 energy for everyone, every day.
- **Walls:** fewer gnats.
- **Library:** town news and the raid report.

The **Library** can draft a raid report for your community: a post with the week's top raiders and your town's status. Select **Open in the editor**, edit it, and publish if you like.

## Seasons

A season has four raid weeks and lasts 28 days. The header shows the season and the day. Days change at 00:00 UTC. After day 28 the game rests until the next season starts.

Karma, badges, trophies and your count of pests chased off stay with you. Shards, powers and the town start fresh each season.

## Leaderboards and rewards

- **This week's raiders** shows who hit the pest hardest.
- **Alliance leaderboard** ranks alliances for the season in Small, Medium and Large leagues.
- Your **Profile and badges** page shows your karma, badges and season trophies.

Raidstead pays no Points, HIVE or tokens. The rewards are badges, trophies and karma. Points are only spent in the game (on rallies and the alliance war chest), and every Point spent is burned. When an alliance's war chest reaches 1,000 Points, everyone in it gets +1 damage for 24 hours. There is a daily limit on how many Points you can spend in the game.
