title: Tips
description: Tip authors on Ecency with Points, HIVE, HBD or Hive Engine tokens, on posts, comments and waves, on the web and in the mobile app.
---

A tip is a direct thank-you to an author, on top of votes. It goes straight to their wallet.

## Send a tip

- **On the web:** click **Tip** (the gift icon) on a post, comment or wave. The transfer form opens with **100 Points** filled in and the memo `Tip for @author/permlink`. Change the amount or the token if you like, then confirm.
- **In the mobile app:** tap the gift icon (with the tip count) under a post or comment to open **Send Tip** (**Support @author**). Pick **HIVE** (default), **HBD**, **POINTS** or any Hive Engine token you hold, enter the amount and confirm (**Send {amount} {currency} to @author?**).

Tips are transfers, so they need your **active** key or a signer such as Keychain or HiveSigner. Hive transfers can't be undone, so check the amount before you confirm.

## See tips

The number next to the gift icon is how many tips a post got. On the web, clicking it on a post that already has tips shows the totals per token and a **Send a tip** button. On waves you tipped, the gift icon's tooltip says **You tipped this**. In the mobile app the list shows **Tips Received**.

## Errors

On the web, a tip uses the normal transfer form, so you see the usual transfer messages, such as **Insufficient funds**. See [Common errors and fixes](/common/errors/#rewards-and-wallet).

In the mobile app, for any token including **POINTS**:

- **Failed to send tip**: the transfer didn't go through. Check your balance and try again.
- **Please enter a valid amount**: enter a number above zero, with no more decimals than the token allows.
- **Insufficient balance**: you don't have enough of that token. Pick another token or a smaller amount.
