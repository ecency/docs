The Discover page helps you find people and communities worth following. Open it from **Discover** in the top menu, or go to [ecency.com/discover](https://ecency.com/discover).

## What's on the Discover page

### Communities

A selection of popular communities, with a link to **Explore more communities** that opens the full list at [ecency.com/communities](https://ecency.com/communities).

### Leaderboard (most active)

The most active users for the period you pick: **Daily**, **Weekly** or **Monthly**. The ranking is based on activities, which counts the posts and comments a user made in that period. The list also shows each user's rewards. On the daily list, a badge marks users who completed all their daily quests that day (UTC).

### Curators

The top curators for the same periods, with their curation rewards and the number of votes they cast.

### Contributors

People who have contributed to Ecency through translation, ideas, development, design or spreading the word. If you'd like to help too, see [Contribution](/contribution/).

## Communities

Communities are groups of people with a shared interest who post, discuss and support each other's content. You can join as many communities as you like.

### Find and join a community

1. Open [ecency.com/communities](https://ecency.com/communities).
2. Sort the list by **Hot**, **Rank**, **Members** or **New**, or search for a topic.
3. Click **Join** on any community.

Learn more in [Communities](/hive/communities/).

### Publish in a community

When you write a post, choose where to publish it: your own blog or one of the communities you joined. The community ID becomes your first tag. Read the community's rules before you post.

### Leave a community

Open the community and hover over the **Joined** button. It changes to **Leave**; click it. The community is removed from your list and its posts stop appearing in your **Communities** feed.
