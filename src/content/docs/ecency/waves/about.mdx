Waves are Ecency's home for short-form content. Share a quick update, a photo, a link or a poll without writing a full post. Open Waves from the top menu or go to [ecency.com/waves](https://ecency.com/waves).

## Post a wave

1. Open [ecency.com/waves](https://ecency.com/waves) and sign in.
2. Type your wave in the box at the top. A wave can be up to 250 characters (replies up to 750).
3. Add extras from the toolbar if you like (see below).
4. Click **Publish it!**

<Screenshot src={shotWaves} alt="The Waves page with the wave composer at the top, the For you feed and trending hashtags on the right" caption="The Waves page: the composer on top, the feed below and trending tags on the right. Media is blurred in this screenshot." />


Very short waves don't earn Points. The form tells you when a wave is too short.

### Tags

Add hashtags like `#photography` in your text. Click a hashtag on any wave to see the waves that use it. Check **What's happening** for the trending hashtags of the last few hours.

### Images, videos and memes

Use the toolbar to add an image, upload a short video, create an [AI image](/ecency/ai-images/) or make a meme.

### Polls

Use the poll button to attach a poll to your wave. It works the same way as polls in posts. See [Publish](/ecency/publish/#creating-polls).

## Reply to a wave

Click **Reply** under a wave to answer it. Open a wave to see the full conversation and use **Hide replies** to fold it again.

## Feeds and sources

At the top of the Waves page you can switch between:

- **For you:** waves from across Hive.
- **Following:** waves from the people you follow.
- **Custom feeds:** click **Add feed** to pin tags or sources (like Threads or Snaps) as extra tabs. They are saved on your device.

Waves gathers short content from several Hive apps into one feed, so you can follow it all in one place:

| Source | From |
|---|---|
| **Waves** | Ecency. New waves are posted to `hive.flow`, with `ecency.waves` as the fallback. |
| **Threads** | INLEO (`leothreads`) |
| **Snaps** | PeakD (`peak.snaps`) |
| **Moments** | Liketu (`liketu.moments`) |
| **Shorts** | Short videos from across these sources |

The unified feed also includes Liketu Speak voice posts (`liketu.speak`).

Every wave is a normal Hive comment, so it earns rewards and keeps working in any app that supports these hosts.

## Promote a wave

Want more views? You can promote a wave with Points, just like a post. See [Boost and promote](/ecency/boost-and-promote/).

## Waves in Decks

Add a **Waves** column to your [Deck](/ecency/decks/about/) to follow waves next to your other feeds.

## Waves mobile app

Prefer your phone? The Ecency team maintains a dedicated Waves app for Android and iOS. Download it on [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.ecency.waves) or the [Apple App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/waves-short-content-sharing/id6504257744). You can also post and read waves in the main [Ecency mobile app](/guides/use-mobile-app/).
