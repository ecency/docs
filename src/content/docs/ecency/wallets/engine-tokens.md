---
title: Hive Engine tokens
description: How to show, send, stake, delegate, trade and claim Hive Engine tokens in the Ecency wallet.
---

Hive Engine is a second-layer token platform on Hive. Communities and projects use it to issue their own tokens. The Ecency wallet lets you hold and manage these tokens next to HIVE, HBD and Points.

SPK Network tokens (SPK, LARYNX and LP) are no longer supported in the Ecency wallet and are hidden from it.

## Show tokens in your wallet

Your wallet always shows Points, HIVE, Hive Power and HBD. Hive Engine tokens appear only after you pick them.

**Web**

1. Open your wallet (your profile, then **Wallet**).
2. Select **Manage tokens**.
3. In **Pick tokens to show**, find the token under **Hive engine** or use **Search token**.
4. Select the tokens you want and **Save**.

Your choice is saved to your Hive profile, so it follows you to other devices.

**Mobile**

1. Open **Wallet** and tap **Add / Remove Assets**.
2. Move tokens from **Available Assets** to **Selected Assets**.

Select a token to open its page with your balances, its history and the available actions.

## Token actions

Open a token from your own wallet to see its actions. Which ones appear depends on the token, because each token sets its own rules (for example, whether it can be staked or delegated and how long unstaking takes).

| Action | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Transfer** | Send liquid tokens to another Hive account. |
| **Stake** | Lock tokens to earn passive rewards. |
| **Unstake** | Release staked tokens. You stop earning passive rewards on them. The token decides how long this takes. |
| **Delegate** | Lend staked tokens to another account. |
| **Undelegate** | Take a delegation back to your own balance. |

To use an action:

- **Web:** select the action card, fill in the account and amount, then confirm.
- **Mobile:** tap the action button on the token page, fill in the form and confirm.

Transfer, stake, unstake and delegate are Hive Engine transactions on the Hive chain, so they need your active key. See [Hive accounts and keys](/hive/hive-accounts/).

## Claim token rewards

If a token has rewards waiting for you, a claim button appears with the amount, for example "0.123 LEO".

- **Web:** select the button on the token in your wallet list or on the token page. It shows "Collecting..." and then "Collected ... !".
- **Mobile:** tap the claim button on the token's card in your wallet.

Claiming uses your posting key. Each token is claimed separately.

## Trade tokens

On the web, the **Trade tokens** button on your wallet opens the market. The **Hive Engine market** lets you trade Hive Engine tokens with limit orders, priced in SWAP.HIVE. It shows buy and sell orders, recent trades and your open orders. You need to be signed in to trade. See [Market](/ecency/market/) for more.

The mobile app has no Hive Engine market. Its swap screen covers HIVE and HBD only.

## Estimated value

Ecency shows an estimated value for each token, in the currency you picked in [settings](/ecency/settings/).

- The value is based on the token's last trade price on the Hive Engine market, converted with the current HIVE price.
- A token with no market price shows a value of 0.
- On the web token page, the balance in your currency counts only your liquid balance, not staked tokens.

Treat this as a guide only. Many Hive Engine tokens trade thinly, so you may not be able to sell at the shown price.
