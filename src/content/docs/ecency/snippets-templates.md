---
title: Snippets and templates
description: Save reusable pieces of text as snippets and whole post setups as templates, and use them in the Ecency editor on web and mobile.
---

Ecency has two ways to reuse what you write often:

- **Snippets** are short pieces of text, such as a signature, a footer with your links or a standard disclaimer. You insert them into a post or comment where your cursor is.
- **Templates** are whole post setups: title, body, tags and post settings. You start a new post from one.

Both are saved to your Ecency account, not to the blockchain, so they are private to you and available on the web and in the mobile app. You need to be logged in to use them.

## Snippets

### Create, edit and delete snippets on the web

1. Open your account menu and choose **Snippets**.
2. Click **Add** (or **Create your first snippet** if you have none).
3. Enter a **Title** and a **Body**, then save with **Add**.

In the same window you can search snippets by title (**Search by title**), and use the menu on each snippet to **Edit** or **Delete** it.

### Use a snippet on the web

In the post editor, click **Snippets** in the toolbar and pick one. Its text is inserted at your cursor. The comment editor has the same toolbar button.

### Snippets on mobile

In the editor, tap the snippets button in the toolbar. The list shows your saved snippets:

- Tap a snippet to insert it.
- Tap **Snippet** to add a new one. Give it a title and a body, then tap **SAVE**.
- Use the icons on each snippet to edit or delete it. Deleting asks you to confirm.

## Templates

### Save a template

Write a post the way you want to reuse it, with its title, body, tags and settings such as rewards, beneficiaries or a poll.

- **Web:** open the **⋮** menu in the editor's top bar and choose **Save as template**. Give it a **Template name** (for example "Weekly update") and click **Save**.
- **Mobile:** open **Post Options** in the editor and tap **Save as template**, then enter a name.

You'll see **Template saved** when it is stored.

### Start a post from a template

- **Web:** open the **⋮** menu in the editor and choose **Templates**. Search by name if you have many, then click **Apply**. If your editor already has content, Ecency asks before replacing it (**Replace your current draft content?**).
- **Mobile:** open **Drafts** from the side menu and go to the **Templates** tab. Tap a template to start a new post from it.

### Delete a template

In the templates list, use **Delete** on the template you no longer need and confirm. To change a template, apply it, make your changes and save it again as a new template, then delete the old one.

### Make it your own

Templates are a starting point. If you try to publish a post that is still mostly the template's text, the web editor warns you: **This post is still mostly your template. Add your own content before publishing.** Posting near-identical content again and again can look like spam to readers and curators, so always add something new.
