The Ecency **Publish** page at [ecency.com/publish](https://ecency.com/publish) is a rich editor for writing posts. You can format with the toolbar or keyboard shortcuts, drag and drop media, add polls and fine-tune rewards before you publish. Prefer Markdown? Use **Classic Editor** in the menu.

## Adding media

### Images

- Drag and drop images into the editor, or use the **Image** button to upload from your device, pick from your gallery, or add an image by link.
- Use the **GIF** button to search for a GIF and **Emoji** to add emoji.
- To create images with AI, use the **AI Image** button. See [AI images](/ecency/ai-images/).

### Videos

- **Upload a video:** use the **Video** button and choose **Upload and paste**. Your video is uploaded to 3Speak and embedded in the post. You can also record a clip with your camera.
- **Add a video by link:** paste a YouTube, 3Speak, Twitch or Vimeo link and it is embedded automatically. Other videos can be added as a regular link.
- **Video or Short:** when you upload, choose **Video** or **Short** under **Video type**. Waves videos are always Shorts.
- **Limits:** a video can be up to 1 GB. In the mobile app (**Add Video**, **Record Video** or **Choose from Library**), Shorts can be up to 60 seconds and a post can have one video.
- **Rewards:** posts with a 3Speak video include an 11% beneficiary for `threespeakfund`, which pays for 3Speak's video hosting.

### Memes

Use **Make a meme** to open the DecentMemes meme maker and drop a meme into your post. Adding a meme routes a small beneficiary reward to the meme creator, plus 1% to Ecency.

## Creating polls

1. Click the **Poll** button in the toolbar. In the mobile app, use the poll option in the editor.
2. Enter your question and add as many choices as you need. Every choice must be filled in and different.
3. Optionally change the settings:
   - **End time**: 7 days from now by default.
   - **Account age for voting(in days)**: only accounts older than this can vote. The poll shows **Only accounts older than N days allowed**.
   - **Max choices voted by user**.
   - **Changing a vote**: let voters change their vote while the poll is open.
   - **Hide votes**: hide who voted from everyone except you.
   - **Interpretation**: count results **By number of votes** or **By tokens** (weighted by stake).
4. Click **Attach to post**.

Voters see results after they vote or when the poll ends. In the mobile app, **Poll not ready for vote, try again shortly** means the poll is still loading. Wait a moment and try again.

## Writing tools

### AI assist

Use **AI Assist** to improve your writing, check grammar, generate a title, suggest tags or write a short recap. You get free uses each day, then it costs Points. See [AI assist](/ecency/ai-assist/).

### Dictation

Use **Dictate** to speak your post and have it transcribed into the editor. Dictation costs Points based on the length of the recording. See [AI assist](/ecency/ai-assist/).

### Translate

Use the translate button to add a machine translation of your post to the end of it. Review and edit the translation before you publish.

### Snippets and templates

- **Snippets** are reusable pieces of text, like a signature or a footer. Insert one from the **Snippets** button in the toolbar.
- **Templates** save a whole post (title, tags and settings) so you can start your next one from it. Use **Templates** in the menu, or **Save as template**.

See [Snippets and templates](/ecency/snippets-templates/).

### Location

Use **Geo tag** to attach a place to your post. Search for a city or place, or use your current location, then fine-tune the pin on the map. This is done in collaboration with WorldMapPin.

## Importing content

You can import an existing blog post or article into the editor instead of starting from scratch. This is useful when you want to republish your own content from another platform on Hive.

### How to import

1. Open the [Import page](https://ecency.com/publish/import) directly, or choose **Import** in the Publish menu.
2. Paste the URL of the article you want to import.
3. The content, title, images and tags are loaded into the editor, where you can review and edit them before publishing.

### Supported sources

- **Hive posts:** URLs from Ecency, PeakD, Hive.blog and other Hive front ends. The original Markdown and tags are preserved.
- **External articles:** URLs from any public website. The article text and images are extracted automatically. A source attribution is added at the end.

:::caution
Only import content that you own or have permission to republish.
:::

## Tags, title and limits

- **Tags:** up to 10. Each tag is at most 24 characters, uses lowercase letters, numbers and at most one dash, starts with a letter and ends with a letter or number. Separate tags with a space. The first tag is the main category. When you post to a community, the community comes first.
- **Title:** up to 255 characters. The short description can be up to 350 characters.
- **Publishing:** **Continue** stays greyed out until you have a title and some text or media, and all images have finished uploading. You need at least one tag to publish.
- **Communities:** some communities only let members post. In the mobile app you'll see **Only members of (community) community can publish post**.

### Image upload errors

- **Image size is too large, try smaller size image again.** Use a smaller image or compress it.
- **Too many uploads. Please wait a moment and try again.** You uploaded many images quickly. Wait a minute.
- **Your session expired. Please refresh the page and try again.** Reload the page and log in again if asked.
- **Image upload is temporarily unavailable. Please try again.** Click **Retry**.
- **You have image with this filename already. Select it from gallery instead.** Pick it from your image gallery.
- In the mobile app you can pick up to 5 images at a time. **File size too big, please resize or upload smaller image** means the image is too large.

## Before you publish

Open the menu in the editor's top bar for more settings.

### Rewards and beneficiaries

- **Reward settings:** choose how your post rewards are paid out. See [Reward options](#reward-options) below.
- **Beneficiaries:** share part of your post rewards with other accounts. See [Custom beneficiary](/hive/custom-beneficiary/).
- **Support Ecency:** on the final step you may see a chip that says **Add 5% beneficiary to support Ecency**. Click it to add @ecency as a beneficiary, or ignore it. You can set a default in your settings.

Some communities ask for a share of post rewards. If a community asks for 25% or more, you'll see a notice before you publish.

### Reward options

On the web, open **Reward settings** in the editor menu. In the mobile app, tap the gear icon at the top of the editor to open **Post Options**, then pick **Reward**. There are three choices:

| Web label | Mobile label | What you get |
|---|---|---|
| **Default 50% / 50%** | **Stake 50%** | Half of your author reward is paid as Hive Power and half as liquid tokens (HBD, or HIVE when the HBD print rate is reduced). This is the default. |
| **Stake 100%** | **Stake 100%** | Your whole author reward is paid as Hive Power. Choose this to grow your stake, votes and Resource Credits faster. |
| **Decline Earnings** | **Decline Reward** | The post pays nothing from the reward pool. Votes still count toward ranking and trending, but neither you nor the curators who vote are paid. |

When to use each option:

- **Default:** you want some liquid tokens you can spend, save or send.
- **Stake 100%:** you are building your account and don't need liquid tokens yet. Hive Power can be [powered down](/guides/power-up-down/) later over 13 weeks.
- **Decline:** announcements, giveaways, contest results, fundraisers or posts where you don't want to take from the shared reward pool.

Good to know:

- The choice applies to that post only, and Ecency sets it when you publish. Hive only allows making it stricter later, so a declined post can never be switched back to paying.
- Curators always receive their half as Hive Power, whatever you pick (unless you decline).
- Replies to a declined post can still earn rewards.
- Rewards are paid 7 days after publishing. See [Curation and rewards](/hive/curation-and-rewards/) and [Payouts and conversions](/ecency/wallets/payouts-and-conversions/).

### Schedule a post

Instead of publishing right away, open **Schedule settings** in the menu and pick a future date and time. See [Scheduled posts](/ecency/scheduled-posts/).

### Drafts

Your work is saved as a draft automatically. You can also **Save draft** at any time and find it later under **Drafts**. See [Notifications, drafts and bookmarks](/ecency/notifications-drafts-bookmarks/).

## Edit and delete posts

**Edit:** open your post's three-dot menu and choose **Edit** (or **Classic Edit** for the Markdown editor). You can edit a post or comment at any time, also after it has paid out. Editing doesn't change its rewards. Every earlier version stays on the blockchain and anyone can see it in **Edit History**. See [Edit history](/common/posting/#edit-history).

**Delete:** **Delete** shows in the menu only when Hive allows it. A post or comment can be deleted only when all of these are true:

- It is yours.
- It has no replies.
- Its votes add up to zero or less (no net upvotes).
- It hasn't paid out yet (it is less than 7 days old).

Otherwise Hive refuses with messages like **Cannot delete a content with replies.**, **Cannot delete a content with positive pending rewards.** or **Content after their payout cannot be deleted.** In that case, edit the post instead, for example to remove the text. A deleted post also disappears from Ecency, but blockchain explorers still keep the original data.

## Share to a community

Already published and want the post to reach another community? Open the post's three-dot menu and choose **Cross post**. Pick one of your communities and add a message. Rewards are automatically declined for cross posts. The discussion follows the host community's rules.
