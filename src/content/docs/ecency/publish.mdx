The Ecency **Publish** page at [ecency.com/publish](https://ecency.com/publish) is a rich editor for writing posts. You can format with the toolbar or keyboard shortcuts, drag and drop media, add polls, and fine-tune rewards before you publish. Prefer Markdown? Use **Back to Classic Editor** in the menu.

## Adding media

### Images

- Drag and drop images into the editor, or use the **Image** button to upload from your device, pick from your gallery, or add an image by link.
- Use the **GIF** button to search for a GIF, and **Emoji** to add emoji.
- To create images with AI, use the **AI Image** button. See [AI images](/ecency/ai-images/).

### Videos

- **Upload a video:** use the **Video** button and choose **Upload and paste**. Your video is uploaded to 3Speak and embedded in the post. You can also record a clip with your camera.
- **Add a video by link:** paste a YouTube, 3Speak, Twitch or Vimeo link and it is embedded automatically. Other videos can be added as a regular link.

### Memes

Use **Make a meme** to open the DecentMemes meme maker and drop a meme into your post. Adding a meme routes a small beneficiary reward to the meme creator, plus 1% to Ecency.

## Creating polls

1. Click the **Poll** button in the toolbar.
2. Enter your question and add as many choices as you need (no duplicates).
3. Optionally set an end time, a minimum account age for voters, and how many choices each voter can pick.
4. Click **Attach to post**.

## Writing tools

### AI assist

Use **AI Assist** to improve your writing, check grammar, generate a title, suggest tags or write a short recap. You get free uses each day, then it costs Points. See [AI assist](/ecency/ai-assist/).

### Dictation

Use **Dictate** to speak your post and have it transcribed into the editor. Dictation costs Points based on the length of the recording. See [AI assist](/ecency/ai-assist/).

### Translate

Use the translate button to add a machine translation of your post to the end of it. Review and edit the translation before you publish.

### Snippets and templates

- **Snippets** are reusable pieces of text, like a signature or a footer. Insert one from the **Snippets** button in the toolbar.
- **Templates** save a whole post (title, tags and settings) so you can start your next one from it. Use **Templates** in the menu, or **Save as template**.

See [Snippets and templates](/ecency/snippets-templates/).

### Location

Use **Geo tag** to attach a place to your post. Search for a city or place, or use your current location, then fine-tune the pin on the map. This is done in collaboration with WorldMapPin.

## Importing content

You can import an existing blog post or article into the editor instead of starting from scratch. This is useful when you want to republish your own content from another platform on Hive.

### How to import

1. Open the [Import page](https://ecency.com/publish/import) directly, or choose **Import** in the Publish menu.
2. Paste the URL of the article you want to import.
3. The content, title, images and tags are loaded into the editor, where you can review and edit them before publishing.

### Supported sources

- **Hive posts:** URLs from Ecency, PeakD, Hive.blog and other Hive front ends. The original Markdown and tags are preserved.
- **External articles:** URLs from any public website. The article text and images are extracted automatically, and a source attribution is added at the end.

:::caution
Only import content that you own or have permission to republish.
:::

## Before you publish

Open the menu in the editor's top bar for more settings.

### Rewards and beneficiaries

- **Reward settings:** choose how your post rewards are paid out.
- **Beneficiaries:** share part of your post rewards with other accounts. See [Custom beneficiary](/hive/custom-beneficiary/).
- **Support Ecency:** on the final step you may see a chip that says **Add 5% beneficiary to support Ecency**. Click it to add @ecency as a beneficiary, or ignore it. You can set a default in your settings.

Some communities ask for a share of post rewards. If a community asks for 25% or more, you'll see a notice before you publish.

### Schedule a post

Instead of publishing right away, open **Schedule settings** in the menu and pick a future date and time. See [Scheduled posts](/ecency/scheduled-posts/).

### Drafts

Your work is saved as a draft automatically. You can also **Save draft** at any time and find it later under **Drafts**. See [Notifications, drafts and bookmarks](/ecency/notifications-drafts-bookmarks/).

## Share to a community

Already published and want the post to reach another community? Open the post's three-dot menu and choose **Cross post**. Pick one of your communities and add a message. Rewards are automatically declined for cross posts, and the discussion follows the host community's rules.
