AI Assist is a writing helper built into the Ecency editor. It can polish your draft, fix grammar, suggest tags, propose titles and write a short summary. Each use costs a few [Ecency Points](/ecency/ecency-points/), and everyone gets a small free allowance every day.

## Where to find it

- **Web:** in the post editor ([ecency.com/publish](https://ecency.com/publish)), click the **AI** button in the toolbar (tooltip **AI Assist**).
- **Mobile:** in the post editor, tap the toolbar button with the small **AI** badge.

You need to be logged in, because uses are paid from your Points balance.

## Actions

Write your content first, then open AI Assist and pick what you want to do:

| Action | What it does | Cost |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Improve Writing** | Polishes grammar, clarity and style | 50 Points |
| **Check Grammar** | Fixes grammar and spelling | 10 Points |
| **Suggest Tags** | Suggests relevant tags for your post | 10 Points |
| **Generate Title** | Gives you three title options to choose from | 10 Points |
| **Recap** | Writes a short TL;DR summary | 30 Points |

These are the prices at the time of writing. The dialog always shows the current **Cost** and your **Balance** before you run an action.

Your text needs at least 50 characters for Improve Writing and Check Grammar, and at least 100 characters for the other actions. The maximum is 10,000 characters.

## Free daily uses

Every account gets **1 free use of each action per day**. The dialog shows how many free uses you have left today. [Ecency Pro](/ecency/ecency-pro/) members get **5 free uses of each action per day**. The free allowance resets at midnight UTC.

## Using the result

When the result is ready you can:

- **Apply to post:** for Improve Writing, Check Grammar and Recap, this **replaces your post text** with the result. Generate Title sets the title you picked, and Suggest Tags adds the tags.
- **Copy** the result and paste it where you want it. This is the safer choice for Recap if you want to keep your full post and add a summary.
- **Try Again** or **Try Another Action**.

On the web, **Show changes** highlights what Improve Writing or Check Grammar changed before you apply it.

If a request fails, your Points are refunded. If your text is rejected by the content policy, the Points are not refunded.

## Recap a post you are reading

On the web, logged-in readers see an **AI Recap** box next to a post's word count and read time. Click **Start** to get a short summary of the post. It uses the same Recap action and allowance as the editor, and you can translate the summary from the result window.

## Dictation

You can also speak instead of type. In the editor toolbar, click the microphone (**Dictate**) on the web, or tap the dictation button with the **AI** badge on mobile. Record up to 5 minutes, then stop to transcribe it into your post.

Dictation is charged by length: 15 Points per 30 seconds of audio, rounded up. The dialog shows the running cost while you record. Ecency Pro members get one free 30-second block per day.

## AI usage disclosure

You can tell readers how AI helped with a post. On the web, open the **⋮** menu in the editor's top bar and choose **AI usage**, then tick **AI writing assistance** and/or **AI-generated media**. Ecency ticks these for you when you apply an AI Assist edit or insert an [AI image](/ecency/ai-images/), and you can change them before publishing. Disclosure is optional.

Posts with a disclosure show a small badge, **AI usage disclosed by author**. The disclosure is saved in a shared format, so other Hive apps can show it too.
