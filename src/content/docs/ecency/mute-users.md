---
title: Mute users
description: Mute accounts you don't want to see on Ecency, understand community mutes, and learn how muting differs from chat privacy settings.
---

Muting lets you hide an account's posts and comments from your own view on Ecency. It is different from a community mute, which moderators use to hide content inside their community. This page explains both.

## Mute an account

- **Web:** open the person's profile and click **Mute** next to the follow button.
- **Mobile:** open the person's profile and tap **MUTE / BLOCK**, or use **Mute / Block** in the menu of one of their posts.

A mute is saved on the Hive blockchain as part of your follow list. Keep in mind:

- **It is public.** Anyone can see which accounts you have muted.
- **It works across Hive.** Other Hive apps that respect mutes hide the account too.
- **It replaces a follow.** Hive keeps one relationship per account, so muting someone also unfollows them, and following a muted account unmutes it.
- **It needs a transaction.** Like following, muting is signed with your posting authority and uses a small amount of [Resource Credits](/hive/resource-credits/).

Because a mute is public, the other person may find out. Ecency can show them a notification that you muted them, in the same way it shows new followers. They can still see and reply to your content: muting only changes what you see.

## What muted content looks like

Once you mute an account, Ecency hides its content for you:

- **Feeds:** their posts are removed from your feeds and lists, as if they weren't there.
- **Comments:** their comments are collapsed. On the web, a collapsed comment is marked **Muted author** and you can click it to reveal the comment. In the mobile app, tap **Muted! Tap To Reveal**.
- **Waves:** their waves are replaced with a **Muted author's post** placeholder.
- **Their profile:** you see a notice, **You muted @name**, explaining that their posts and comments are hidden from you.

Because muting is read from the blockchain after the page loads, a muted author's post may appear for a moment before it disappears.

## Unmute an account

Ecency doesn't have a separate page listing everyone you muted. To unmute someone, open their profile and click **Unmute** (web) or tap **UNMUTE / UNBLOCK** (mobile). Their content shows up again right away.

## Community mutes

Communities have their own moderation, separate from your personal mutes.

- **Muting a post:** community moderators (and admins and owners) can open the **⋮** menu on a post in their community and choose **Mute**. They can add **Notes** explaining the decision. **Unmute** restores the post.
- **Muted role:** moderators can give an account the **Muted** role in their community. New posts from that account in the community are muted automatically.

A post muted by a community is not deleted from Hive. On the web, readers see a warning that the content was muted by the community's moderators for not following community guidelines, and can choose to reveal it. In the mobile app the post shows **Content muted for community guidelines violation** with **Tap to reveal**.

See [Moderate a community](/guides/moderate-community/) for the moderator side.

## The Muted feed

Hive hides some content from normal feeds, for example posts from accounts with a very low reputation. The **Muted** option in the feed menu (at [ecency.com/muted](https://ecency.com/muted)) shows these posts so you can review them. It is not a list of the accounts you muted.

## Muting and chats

A Hive mute hides posts and comments. It is not a chat block. To control who can message you in [Chats](/ecency/chats/), use the **Chat Privacy** setting:

- **Everyone**
- **Only people I follow**
- **No one (disable DMs)**

Since muting someone also unfollows them, choosing **Only people I follow** stops accounts you muted from starting a DM with you. Inside chats, **Mute channel** only silences alerts from that conversation. It doesn't hide the other person.
