A reblog shares someone else's post on your own blog. When you reblog a post, it appears on your blog and in your followers' feeds, so they can enjoy it too. Reblogging is a good way to support authors you like.

## How to reblog a post

1. Find the **reblog** button (the two-arrow icon) in the footer of the post.
2. Click it. Ecency asks you to confirm: "Reblog to @yourname's blog?".
3. Click **Reblog**.

The post now appears on your blog. Reblogging uses your posting key.

## Undo a reblog

If you change your mind, click the reblog button on the same post again and choose **Undo**. The post is removed from your blog.

## Who reblogged a post

- The number next to the reblog button shows how many accounts have reblogged the post.
- When someone reblogs your post, you get a notification.
- In your feed, reblogged posts are marked with who reblogged them. You can show or hide reblogs from people you follow with the feed filter. See [feed](/ecency/feed/).

## Points for reblogging

Each reblog earns you **1 Ecency Point**. See [Ecency Points](/ecency/ecency-points/) for other ways to earn.

## Reblog your community post to your blog

Posts you publish in a [community](/hive/communities/) appear in the community, not on your blog. When you publish in a community, the editor shows a **Reblog** toggle ("Auto share with your followers"). Turn it on and Ecency reblogs your new post to your own blog right after publishing, so your followers see it too.
