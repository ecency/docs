---
title: Resource Credits troubleshooting
description: Diagnose low Resource Credits, restore your ability to post and estimate RC needs for common actions.
---

Resource Credits (RC) power every Hive transaction. When they run low, you may be unable to post, comment, vote or claim rewards. Use this guide to recover quickly. For the basics, see [Resource Credits](/hive/resource-credits/).

## Check your RC status

1. Open your profile on Ecency. Your RC level is shown on your profile card, under your avatar and username.
2. Click **Resource Credits** to see your available and used RC, your delegations in and out, plus how many posts or comments, votes, transfers and reblogs or follows you can still make.
3. If your RC is low, check whether you recently made many transactions, like a lot of edits or comments in a short time.

Ecency also shows a warning next to the publish, comment and vote buttons when your RC is probably too low for that action to go through.

## Fast ways to restore RC

- **Top up RC with Points**: a short-term RC boost for your own account. See below.
- **Power up** some HIVE to increase your RC. See [power up and down](/guides/power-up-down/).
- Use **[Boost+](/ecency/boost-and-promote/)** to get a temporary Hive Power delegation with Points, which also adds RC.
- Ask a friend to **delegate RC or Hive Power** to your account. See [delegate RC](/guides/delegate-rc/) and [delegate HP](/guides/delegate-hp/).
- Wait: RC refills by about **20% per day**, fully in about 5 days.

## RC top-up with Points

1. When the low RC warning appears, click **Top up RC**.
2. Pick a duration. The current price is shown in the app.
3. Confirm the purchase. The extra RC appears on your account shortly.

You can only have one active top-up at a time. If you don't have enough Points, you can [buy Points](/ecency/buy-points/).

## Reduce RC usage

- Limit **edits** until your RC recovers. Every edit is a new transaction.
- Avoid posting many times in a short period. Comment and vote more sparingly.
- Turn off automatic posting from third-party apps if they are using up your RC.

## Estimate RC needs (typical ranges)

| Action | Approximate RC cost |
| --- | --- |
| Vote or short comment | Very low |
| New post | Moderate, depends on length |
| Token transfer | Low to moderate |
| Power up or down | Low |

Costs change with network usage and the size of the transaction. The estimate on your Resource Credits panel gives you a personal view.

## When to ask for help

- Your RC stays near zero for more than a day even after powering up.
- You see errors signing transactions even though you have RC.
- You suspect unusual RC use from a compromised key. In that case, also see [manage keys](/guides/manage-keys/).

Ask in the [Ecency Help chat](https://ecency.com/chats/hive-125126/channel) with a screenshot of your Resource Credits panel.
