Ecency is built on the [Hive blockchain](https://hive.io), so your activity can earn real tokens. You can also earn Ecency Points for using the app.

## Start earning: first steps

You don't need to invest anything to start. A free account can start earning right away. How much depends on the votes your posts and comments get.

1. **Secure your account.** Save your keys before anything else. See [Create account](/guides/create-account/#save-your-keys).
2. **Introduce yourself.** Write a short introduction post with the `introduction` tag. Members often welcome and vote on new people.
3. **Post original content in an active community.** Pick a [community](/hive/communities/) that fits your topic and read its rules first.
4. **Reply and comment every day.** Comments can earn rewards too. They also earn [Points](#ecency-points) and help people find you.
5. **Vote on posts you like.** Votes can earn a small curation reward. It grows with your Hive Power.
6. **Collect your daily Points.** Check in, spin the wheel and finish [daily quests](/ecency/daily-rewards/).
7. **Power up part of your rewards.** More Hive Power makes your votes worth more and gives you more Resource Credits. See [Power up and down](/guides/power-up-down/).

For a day-by-day plan, see the [onboarding checklist](/get-started/onboarding-checklist/). For more readers, see [Grow your audience](/guides/grow-your-audience/).

## Ways to earn Hive tokens

- **Posting**: share content and receive upvotes from the community. Depending on the votes, you get a share of the reward pool.
- **Voting and curating**: if you upvote a post before it becomes popular, you earn curation rewards. The amount depends on your Hive Power and how early you vote. See [curation and rewards](/hive/curation-and-rewards/).
- **Staking (powering up)**: HIVE that you power up into Hive Power earns a small share of newly created HIVE.
- **HBD savings interest**: Hive Backed Dollars (HBD) in savings earn interest. The rate is set by witnesses, currently about 10% APR. See [HBD savings interest](/guides/hbd-savings-interest/).

You can also buy HIVE or HBD on cryptocurrency exchanges and transfer them to your account.

## How content rewards are split

Content rewards for posts and comments are paid 7 days after publishing:

- **50%** goes to the curators (the voters).
- **50%** goes to the author, normally half as Hive Power and half as HBD.

When the HBD print rate is reduced, the HBD part of the author reward is paid as liquid HIVE instead. The print rate is currently 0, so that part is currently paid in HIVE. Beneficiaries and your payout option, like **Stake 100%**, also change how the author reward is paid.

## What earns the most?

There is no fixed answer. Rewards come from people and curators voting on what they like, so no topic or format is guaranteed to pay. Posts that tend to do well:

- **Original work with visible effort:** your own photos, experiences, tutorials, reviews, analysis or art.
- **Posts that fit an active community**, where interested readers and curators already look.
- **Authors who show up:** they post steadily and answer their readers.

Posts that earn little or get downvoted: copied or recycled content, image-only posts, tag or community abuse (for example unrelated tags or the wrong community) or reward farming. These are the same red flags the curators use. See [How authors get curated](/ecency/curation-desk/#how-authors-get-curated).

To see what is earning right now, open a community feed and sort by **Payouts**. See [Feed](/ecency/feed/#sorts).

## Why did my post earn little or nothing?

This is normal for new accounts. Common reasons:

- **Few people have found you yet.** It takes time to build readers. Engage in communities and comment on other posts.
- **Small votes.** A vote's value depends on the voter's Hive Power. Votes from new accounts are worth very little.
- **Very small payouts are not paid.** Hive doesn't pay out a post or comment whose total reward is below 0.020 HBD in value.
- **Rewards were declined** for the post, or it's a cross post. See [Reward options](/ecency/publish/#reward-options).
- **The post was downvoted or hidden.** Downvotes show in the post's vote list. Esie can tell you whether a post is grayed out or hidden, when it pays out and whether rewards were declined: send it the link in [chat](/ecency/esie/).

The payout shown on a post is an estimate until the 7-day voting window ends. See [When can I claim my rewards?](#when-can-i-claim-my-rewards)

## Should I post or curate?

Both earn, but in different ways:

- **Posting and commenting** are usually the better earner for a new account, because author rewards depend on the votes you get, not your own Hive Power.
- **Curation** pays a share of each post's rewards to its voters. It depends on your Hive Power, so it's small at first and grows as you power up.

Most people do both: they post and comment, vote on good content and power up part of what they earn.

## Ecency Points

Besides Hive tokens, many actions on Ecency earn [Ecency Points](/ecency/ecency-points/), which you can spend inside Ecency, for example to promote posts or boost your account.

| Action | Points |
| --- | --- |
| Publish a post | 15 |
| Write a comment | 5 |
| Reblog a post | 1 |
| First login (one-time bonus) | 10 |
| Check in (stay active in the app) | 0.25 |
| Vote | 0.01 x vote weight |
| Delegate Hive Power to @ecency | about 100 per day for each 1000 HP (paused after 90 days of inactivity) |
| Register your community | 20 for each post published in it with Ecency apps |
| Usage bonus | 10 after every 48 check-ins |
| Free daily spin | 1 to 45, higher with more activity in the last 24 hours |
| Invite friends | see the [referral program](/get-started/referral/) |

The post, comment, reblog and vote amounts are the maximum for your first action of the day; repeats on the same UTC day earn gradually less. Regular use of the app also gives you extra chances to earn bonus Points. Daily check-ins, quests and the spin live on the [daily rewards](/ecency/daily-rewards/) page. You can also [buy Points](/ecency/buy-points/).

## Rewards in your wallet

Your wallet shows three kinds of rewards:

- **Author rewards**: earned by your own posts and comments.
- **Curation rewards**: earned by upvoting posts and comments.
- **Points**: earned for activity on Ecency, like posting, commenting, voting and checking in.

## How do I see my rewards?

On the website, open the **Wallet** tab on your profile. In the mobile app, open **Wallet** from the bottom navigation bar. You'll see your author and curation rewards there.

You can view the same information for other users by visiting their wallet.

## When can I claim my rewards?

Posts and comments stay open for votes for 7 days. During that time, the amount shown is only an estimate (pending payout), because votes and the token price can change. After 7 days, the pending payout becomes an actual reward. Click **Collect all** in your web wallet (or **Claim Reward** in the mobile app) to add the tokens to your account. If something looks stuck, see [payouts and conversions](/ecency/wallets/payouts-and-conversions/).

## Turn rewards into money

1. **Liquid HIVE** can be sent to a crypto exchange and sold there. See [Exchanges: deposit and withdraw](/guides/exchanges/).
2. **Hive Power** must be powered down first. It's paid out as liquid HIVE over 13 weeks. See [Power up and down](/guides/power-up-down/).
3. **HBD** can be converted to HIVE in your wallet (this takes 3.5 days) or traded on the [market](/ecency/market/). See [Payouts and conversions](/ecency/wallets/payouts-and-conversions/).

Whether you can then withdraw to a bank account depends on the exchange and your country.

**Ecency Points can't be cashed out.** They are spent inside Ecency, mostly on growth: [Promote](/ecency/boost-and-promote/), [Boost+](/ecency/boost-and-promote/), RC top-ups and other [perks](/ecency/ecency-points/#what-can-i-use-points-for). You can also [tip](/ecency/tips/) other creators with Points to support them and help them grow.
