---
title: New user onboarding checklist
description: A first-week checklist for new Ecency users, from securing keys to your first Wave, first post, and first earnings.
---

Follow this checklist to set up your Hive identity, secure your keys, and start participating on Ecency in your first week. The fastest way to feel at home is to say something small today, not to write a perfect post next week.

## Day 0: Create and secure your account

- **Create an account** via [Ecency signup](https://ecency.com/signup?referral=ecency) or the [account creation guide](/guides/create-account/).
- Save your **owner**, **active**, **posting**, and **memo** keys in at least two offline places.
- For safer logins, install a signing extension like **Hive Keeper** (by Ecency) or **Hive Keychain**, or use the **Ecency mobile app**. See [login methods](/guides/login-methods/).
- Note your **recovery account** (often `ecency` if created through Ecency) and how to contact them.

## Day 0: Post your first Wave

Your first action on Ecency should take less than a minute. [Waves](/ecency/waves/about/) are short posts, like a quick status update: no title, no formatting pressure, just a sentence or two.

- Open [Waves](https://ecency.com/waves) and **post your first Wave**: who you are, what you are into, or what brought you to Ecency.
- **Vote on a few Waves** you enjoy and reply to one. Conversations start faster in Waves than anywhere else.
- **Follow a handful of people** whose Waves you like, so your feed has life in it from day one.
- Do a **daily check-in** on the [Perks page](https://ecency.com/perks) to start your streak and earn [Ecency Points](/ecency/ecency-points/).

## Day 1 to 2: Learn how Ecency works

- Read [What is Ecency?](/get-started/what-is-ecency/) and [What is Hive?](/hive/what-is-hive/).
- Review [keys and authorities](/hive/hive-accounts/) so you know which key to use for each action.
- Set up your **profile and avatar** so people recognize you.
- Keep the Wave habit going: one Wave and a few votes a day builds your network quickly.

## Day 2 to 3: Get ready to post

- Draft your **introduction post** with relevant tags like `introduction`.
- Check your **Resource Credits (RC)** on your profile card. If they are low, [top up RC with Points](/hive/resource-credits/), do a small [power up](/guides/power-up-down/) or get a temporary [Boost+](/ecency/boost-and-promote/).
- Learn **posting etiquette** and anti-abuse rules in the [content guidelines](/common/posting/).

## Day 3 to 4: Engage and earn

- Publish your introduction post and **reply to comments**.
- Vote on posts to learn how **voting power** regenerates (see [curation and rewards](/hive/curation-and-rewards/)).
- Join 2 or 3 **communities** relevant to your interests (see [Communities](/hive/communities/)).
- Explore **Ecency Points** and how to spend them for visibility in [Ecency Points](/ecency/ecency-points/) and [Boost and Promote](/ecency/boost-and-promote/).

## Day 4 to 5: Wallet basics

- Learn the differences between **HIVE**, **HBD**, and **Hive Power** in [Hive tokens](/common/hive-tokens/).
- Try a small **power up** or **transfer** to understand signing transactions.
- Review **savings** and **conversion** timelines in [payouts and conversions](/ecency/wallets/payouts-and-conversions/).

## Day 5 to 7: Mobile and cross-chain

- Install and sign in on the [Ecency mobile app](/guides/use-mobile-app/) to enable push notifications and quick posting.
- If you hold other assets, check your cross-chain addresses in the [Ecency Wallet](/ecency/wallets/about/) and review the [cross-chain wallets guide](/ecency/wallets/cross-chain-wallets/).

## Ongoing habits

- **Wave daily, post weekly**: short Waves keep you visible and connected between longer posts.
- Keep your **daily check-in streak** alive on the [Perks page](https://ecency.com/perks). Streaks compound into Points and habits. See [daily rewards](/ecency/daily-rewards/).
- Claim rewards weekly and consider **powering up** a portion for more RC and influence.
- Vote on **witnesses** and **DHF proposals** using the [governance participation guide](/hive/governance/).
- Back up your keys any time you rotate them, and bookmark the [account recovery playbook](/guides/recovery/).
- Stay connected via the [Ecency Help chat](https://ecency.com/chats/hive-125126/channel) for support and updates.
