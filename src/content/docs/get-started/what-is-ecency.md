Ecency is a user-friendly social app built on the [Hive blockchain](/hive/what-is-hive/). You can create, share, discover and earn from content. You own your account and everything you publish.

You can use Ecency on the [web](https://ecency.com) and with the [mobile apps](https://ecency.com/mobile) for Android and iOS. You can also install the web version as an app (PWA).

## How Ecency works

Your posts, comments, votes and wallet actions are recorded on the Hive blockchain, so your content stays accessible and censorship resistant. Some Ecency features, such as Points and chats, store their data off-chain on Ecency's servers. When you post, comment or vote, the action is signed with your keys and stored on Hive.

Hive rewards creators and curators with tokens: HIVE, Hive Power and Hive Backed Dollars (HBD). You can trade HIVE and HBD on cryptocurrency exchanges or on the built-in [market](/ecency/market/). Ecency also rewards activity with [Ecency Points](/ecency/ecency-points/), which you can spend inside Ecency.

Learn more about the tokens in [Hive tokens](/common/hive-tokens/) and about rewards in [how to earn](/get-started/earning/).

## What you can do on Ecency

**Social**

- [Feed](/ecency/feed/) and [Discover](/ecency/discover/): follow people and find new content.
- [Waves](/ecency/waves/about/): short posts for quick updates and conversations.
- [Communities](/hive/communities/): topic-based groups with their own rules and moderators.
- [Chats](/ecency/chats/): direct messages and community channels.
- [Decks](/ecency/decks/about/): follow many feeds side by side.
- Email digests: get a summary of new posts from a community or creator by email.

**Create**

- [Publish](/ecency/publish/): a rich editor with drafts, [scheduled posts](/ecency/scheduled-posts/) and [snippets and templates](/ecency/snippets-templates/).
- [AI assist](/ecency/ai-assist/) and [AI images](/ecency/ai-images/): help with writing and images, paid with Points.
- [Listen](/ecency/listen/): unlock an audio version of a post with Points.

**Earn and grow**

- [Ecency Points](/ecency/ecency-points/) and [daily rewards](/ecency/daily-rewards/): check-ins, quests and a daily spin.
- [Boost+ and Promote](/ecency/boost-and-promote/): spend Points on a temporary Hive Power delegation or on more visibility for your posts.
- **Account Boost**: a 30-day Hive Power delegation. Buy it in the mobile app, or start on the [Perks page](https://ecency.com/perks) on the web and scan the QR code with the app. You can boost any account, not just your own.
- [Ecency Pro](/ecency/ecency-pro/): a verified Pro badge, a free blog and early access to new features.
- [Blog hosting](/ecency/blog-hosting/): your Hive blog on your own site.

**Wallet and account**

- [Wallet](/ecency/wallets/about/): view balances, send tokens, power up or down and manage savings.
- Multiple accounts: switch between Hive accounts with ease.
- [Notifications](/ecency/notifications-drafts-bookmarks/): alerts for votes, replies, mentions, rewards and more.
