Invite your friends to Ecency and earn [Ecency Points](/ecency/ecency-points/) when they join and become active. There is no limit on how many people you can refer.

## How it works

1. Share your referral link: `https://ecency.com/signup?referral=USERNAME`, with your own username in place of `USERNAME`.
2. Your friend creates an account with that link.
3. When your friend earns their first **250 Points**, you earn **100 Points**.

## Find your referrals

- **Web**: open `https://ecency.com/@USERNAME/referrals`, or the **Referrals** tab on your profile.
- **Mobile app**: open the side menu and tap **Refer & Earn**.

The referrals page shows:

- A button to copy your referral link
- **Earned Points**: Points you have already received for referrals
- **Pending Points**: Points you can still earn from referrals who haven't reached 250 Points yet
- A table of everyone you referred, with the date their account was created and whether you have been rewarded

## Help your referrals get started

New accounts often have little Hive Power, which means few [Resource Credits](/hive/resource-credits/). From the referrals table, you can click **Delegate HP** next to a referral to delegate Hive Power to them, so they can post, comment and vote more easily. You can remove the delegation later. See [delegate HP](/guides/delegate-hp/).
