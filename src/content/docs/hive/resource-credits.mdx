Hive has no transaction fees. Instead, it uses **Resource Credits (RC)** to share the network fairly and prevent spam. Every action you take on the blockchain, like posting, commenting, voting or transferring tokens, uses a small amount of RC. Reading content does not use any RC.

## How RC works

- RC is **not a token**. You can't buy, sell or transfer it. It's a value tied to your account.
- The more **Hive Power (HP)** you have, the more RC you get.
- RC regenerates automatically by about **20% per day**, so an empty RC pool is full again in about **5 days**.
- Each action costs RC. Bigger transactions, like a long post, cost more than a vote.

## Check your RC

On Ecency, open your profile. Your RC level is shown on your profile card under your avatar and username. Click **Resource Credits** to see:

- How much RC is available and how much is used
- RC delegations you have sent and received
- An estimate of how many posts or comments, votes, transfers and reblogs or follows you can still make

When you are writing a post or comment, or voting, Ecency also warns you if your RC is probably too low for the action to go through.

## Running out of RC

If you run out of RC, new transactions fail until your RC regenerates. You can:

- **Wait**: RC refills by about 20% per day.
- **Top up RC with Points**: see below.
- **Power up HIVE**: more Hive Power means more RC. See [power up and down](/guides/power-up-down/).
- **Get a delegation**: another user can delegate Hive Power or RC to you. Ecency also offers [Boost+](/ecency/boost-and-promote/), a temporary Hive Power delegation paid with Points.

For step-by-step fixes, see [RC troubleshooting](/common/resource-credits/troubleshooting/).

## RC top-up with Points

RC top-up lets you spend [Ecency Points](/ecency/ecency-points/) on a short-term RC boost for your own account, so you can keep posting. It adds RC only, not Hive Power.

1. When Ecency warns you that your RC is low, click **Top up RC**.
2. Choose a duration. The current price for each option is shown in the app.
3. Confirm. Your top-up appears shortly after.

You can have one active top-up at a time. If you need more Points, see [buy Points](/ecency/buy-points/).

## Delegating RC

If you have more RC than you use, you can delegate some of it to another account. This helps friends or new users who have little Hive Power. Delegated RC still belongs to you. You can change or remove the delegation at any time. You can't delegate all of your RC, so you always keep enough to transact yourself.

To delegate RC on Ecency:

1. Open your profile and click **Resource Credits** on your profile card.
2. Click **Delegate RC**.
3. Enter the username and the amount of RC, then confirm.

See [delegate RC](/guides/delegate-rc/) for the full guide.

## New accounts

New accounts with little or no Hive Power have little RC. Some apps, including Ecency, may give new users a small temporary delegation to help them get started. If you still run low, use the options above.

## Summary

- RC replaces transaction fees on Hive.
- More Hive Power means more RC.
- RC regenerates about 20% per day, fully in about 5 days.
- If you run low, wait, top up RC with Points, power up or ask for a delegation.
