---
title: Delegate Resource Credits
description: Share your spare Resource Credits with another Hive account on Ecency, then update or remove the delegation.
---

Resource Credits (RC) are what every Hive action costs: posts, comments, votes and transfers. New accounts often have too little RC to do much. If you have RC to spare, you can delegate some of it to a friend so they can keep posting and voting while they grow their own Hive Power.

RC delegation is done on the Ecency website.

## Why delegate RC

- **Onboard friends.** A brand new account with little Hive Power runs out of RC quickly. A delegation lets them keep using Hive.
- **Support an app or bot account** that makes a lot of small transactions.
- **Keep your Hive Power working for you.** RC delegation does not move any HP.

## RC delegation vs HP delegation

| | RC delegation | HP delegation |
|---|---|---|
| What the recipient gets | More RC, so more transactions | More RC and more voting influence |
| Does HP leave your account? | No | It is lent out until you undelegate |
| Affects your curation rewards? | No | Yes, delegated HP doesn't vote for you |

If you want to lend voting power as well, see [Delegate Hive Power](/guides/delegate-hp/).

## How to delegate RC

1. Log in on [ecency.com](https://ecency.com) and open any profile page, for example your own.
2. On the profile card, click the **Resource Credits** bar (it shows **Available** and **Used**).
3. A window opens with your RC percentage, **Delegations Out**, **Delegations In** and an estimate of how many posts, votes and transfers your RC can cover.
4. Click **Delegate RC**.
5. In **To**, enter the username you want to delegate to. You can enter several usernames separated by commas to give each of them the same amount.
6. In **Amount**, enter the RC amount as a whole number. The form shows your **Balance** in RC.
7. Click **Next**, check the summary, then click **Confirm** and sign the transaction.

### Amount rules

- The minimum delegation is **5,000,000,000 RC** (5 billion). Smaller amounts show "Minimum RC is 5,000,000,000".
- The amount must be lower than your current RC balance, or you see "Insufficient resource credits".
- RC numbers are large because RC is measured in small units. Your own balance is shown in the same units, so compare against it.

## Update or remove a delegation

1. Open your own profile and click the **Resource Credits** bar.
2. Click **Delegations Out** to see the **Outgoing** list. You can search it by username.
3. Next to a delegation:
   - Click **update** to open the delegation form with the current amount filled in. Change the amount and confirm.
   - Click **delete**, then **Confirm** on "Are you sure?". This sets the delegation to 0 and removes it.

Click **Delegations In** to see who is delegating RC to you (the **Incoming** list).

## Good to know

- RC delegation only gives the recipient more transaction capacity. It does not give them HP, votes or rewards.
- The recipient's RC from your delegation regenerates over time, like any other RC.
- You can change the amount at any time by delegating again to the same account.
