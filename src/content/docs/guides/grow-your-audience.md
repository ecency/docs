Growing on Hive takes time. Readers and curators notice people who post steadily, talk with others and show up in the right places. This guide collects what works on Ecency.

## Set up your profile

Your profile is the first thing a new reader checks. Add a profile image, a cover image, a short about text and your website. See [Profile](/ecency/profile/).

Your first post can be an introduction. Many people use the `introduction` tag, so other members can find and welcome you.

## Create content people want to read

- **Be original.** Your own photos, experience and opinion count for more than copied or recycled content.
- **Make it easy to read.** Use a clear title, headings, short paragraphs and images. See [Publish](/ecency/publish/).
- **Use a few relevant tags.** Pick tags that match your topic. Unrelated tags look like spam. See [Content guidelines](/common/posting/).
- **Try different formats.** Add [polls](/ecency/publish/#creating-polls), videos or memes. Writing tools like [AI assist](/ecency/ai-assist/) and [snippets and templates](/ecency/snippets-templates/) help you write faster.
- **Post steadily.** A steady rhythm beats a burst of posts followed by silence. [Scheduled posts](/ecency/scheduled-posts/) let you publish at a set time.
- **Post short updates too.** [Waves](/ecency/waves/about/) are short posts for quick thoughts, questions and photos between your longer posts.

## Find your community

Communities bring together readers who care about the same topic, so a post there reaches people who are already interested.

1. Open [Discover](/ecency/discover/) or [ecency.com/communities](https://ecency.com/communities) and search for your topic.
2. Join the communities you like and read their rules before you post.
3. Publish your post in the community that fits it best. See [Communities](/hive/communities/).

Read and reply to posts in your communities, not only your own. People who give attention usually get attention back.

### Which community pays the most?

There is no fixed list. Rewards change every day. To compare, open a community feed and sort by **Payouts**, which shows the posts with the highest pending rewards. See [Feed](/ecency/feed/#sorts). Pick a community for its readers and topic first. A post that fits its community gets more votes than one posted only for the rewards.

## Engage with people

Engagement is the fastest way to grow.

- **Reply to every comment** on your posts. Curators look for authors who answer their readers.
- **Comment on other posts** with something useful to say. Thoughtful comments get you noticed and raise your [reputation](/common/reputation/).
- **Follow people with similar interests.** Use [Search](/ecency/search/) to find posts on your topic, then follow the authors you enjoy. The Following feed shows their new posts.
- **Vote and reblog** good posts. Votes earn you [curation rewards](/hive/curation-and-rewards/) and [reblogs](/common/reblog/) share a post with your followers.
- **Send a tip** to say thanks for a post you liked. See [Tips](/ecency/tips/).
- **Join community chats.** Many communities have a chat channel where members talk every day. See [Chats](/ecency/chats/).

## Join contests

Communities and accounts on Hive often run contests and challenges, for example weekly photo, writing or topic contests. They are a good way to meet new readers and get votes.

- Look for contest announcements in your communities, or search for `contest` or `challenge` with [Search](/ecency/search/).
- Read the rules: the tag to use, the community to post in, the deadline and how winners are picked.
- Visit and comment on other entries too. That is often where you find new followers.

## Share your posts

- **Post sharing channel:** share a link to your post with a short description in the [Post sharing](https://ecency.com/chats/hive-125126/channel) chat channel, so more readers and communities see it.
- **Share outside Hive:** copy the post's ecency.com link and share it on your other social accounts. The link opens the app if it's installed and the website if not. See [Deep links](/developers/deep-links/).
- **Cross post to a community:** open the post menu and choose **Cross post** to show a post you published in a community in another community you are subscribed to. Posts published only to your blog can't be cross posted. Rewards are declined for cross posts. See [Cross-post to another community](/hive/communities/#cross-post-to-another-community).

## Promote and boost with Points

[Ecency Points](/ecency/ecency-points/) pay for perks that help your posts reach more people.

- **Promote:** show a post or a wave in the Promoted feed and across Ecency for the time you pick.
- **Boost+:** a temporary Hive Power delegation that gives you more Resource Credits and voting influence.
- **RC top-up:** a short Resource Credits boost so you can keep posting and commenting.

See [Boost and Promote](/ecency/boost-and-promote/) to compare them.

You earn Points for activity on Ecency and from [daily rewards](/ecency/daily-rewards/) (check-in, quests and the daily spin). You can also [buy Points](/ecency/buy-points/) by card, with HIVE or HBD, or in the mobile app.

## Use your stats

On the web, open **Insights** on your profile to see **Views**, **Unique views** and **Minutes** (average visit length). The eye icon on a post shows its own stats. Look at which posts readers find and stay on, then do more of what works. See [Insights](/ecency/profile/#insights).

## Get noticed by curators

The Ecency [curation desk](/ecency/curation-desk/) reads new posts from every Hive app. There is no form to submit your own post. Curators look for original work with visible effort, authors who answer replies, posts that fit their community and first posts from new accounts. See [How authors get curated](/ecency/curation-desk/#how-authors-get-curated).

You can also help others: [recommend posts](/ecency/curation-desk/#recommend-a-post-to-the-curators) you liked to the curators.

## Go further with Ecency Pro

[Ecency Pro](/ecency/ecency-pro/) adds tools for creators: email [newsletters](/ecency/newsletters/) to your subscribers, your own [hosted blog](/ecency/blog-hosting/), Insights for any profile and larger AI allowances.

## What to avoid

These shortcuts hurt more than they help. They can bring downvotes, a lower reputation or a mute from a community:

- Copying content without credit
- Using unrelated tags or posting the same content in many places
- The same short comment on many posts
- Asking for votes in comments

See [Content guidelines](/common/posting/).
