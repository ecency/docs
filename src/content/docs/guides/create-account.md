A Hive account is a human-readable username (like `alice` or `bob123`) that lets you post, vote and interact on the Hive blockchain. You can create one on Ecency in a few minutes.

## Sign up on Ecency

Go to [ecency.com/signup](https://ecency.com/signup) and pick one of the options.

### Free account

A full Hive account at no cost, with basic checks so bots can't abuse the free signup.

1. Choose **Free Account**.
2. Enter a username and your email address, then complete the check.
3. Open the email Ecency sends you and verify it to finish signing up. Check your spam folder if you can't find it.

### Premium account

A paid account with a few extras:

- Instant creation, without extra checks or waiting
- 3x the Resource Credits of a normal new account for 90 days
- 300 Ecency Points to help you get started

Choose **Premium Account**, pick a username and pay by card. The current price is shown on the button. If card payment isn't available, you can scan a QR code and finish the purchase in the Ecency mobile app.

### Crypto wallet

Create your account using **MetaMask**, with no seed phrase to manage. This option is only available on desktop browsers, because it needs the MetaMask browser extension. Choose **Crypto Wallet** and follow the steps.

### Invited by a friend

A friend who already has a Hive account can create one for you at no cost to you.

- **If you don't have an account:** choose **Invited by a Friend**, pick a username and send the link or code to your friend.
- **If you have an account and want to onboard a friend:** open the link your friend sent you and pay the account fee with HIVE or with account creation credits.

## Save your keys

When your account is created, you get a set of keys (owner, active, posting and memo). Save them right away in a safe place. Nobody can restore lost keys for you.

- Use your **posting** key for everyday things like posting and voting.
- Keep your **owner** key offline. You only need it to change keys or recover your account.

Learn more in [Hive accounts and keys](/hive/hive-accounts/).

## Tips

- Hive accounts are permanent. You can't delete or rename them.
- Other Hive apps can also create accounts. Your account works in every Hive app.
