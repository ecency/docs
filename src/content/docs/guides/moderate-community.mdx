Once your community exists, you run it from its page on Ecency. This guide covers the team roles, the settings, the day-to-day moderation tools and the Ecency community rewards program. If you haven't created a community yet, start with [Create a community](/guides/create-community/).

Roles, settings, pins and mutes are stored on the Hive blockchain, so other community-aware Hive apps see them too.

## Roles and what they can do

Every account in a community has one role. Ecency shows this summary when you assign a role:

| Role | What it can do |
| --- | --- |
| Owner (the community account, `hive-xxxxxx`) | Assign admins |
| Admin | Edit settings, assign mods |
| Moderator | Mute, pin, set user titles |
| Member | Listed on the leadership team |
| Guest | The default; can post and comment |
| Muted | New posts are automatically muted |

You can only assign roles below your own. An owner can set admin, mod, member, guest or muted. An admin can set mod, member, guest or muted. A moderator can set member, guest or muted.

The community type also matters. In a **Topic** community anyone can post or comment. In a **Journal** only members can post. Guests can only comment. In a **Council** only members can post or comment. Muted accounts can't post or comment in any type.

### Set a role

1. Open your community page and click **Edit roles** in the sidebar card. This opens the **User Roles** page (`ecency.com/roles/hive-xxxxxx`).
2. Click **Add User**, or click an existing role in the list to change it.
3. In **Set user role**, enter the **Username** and pick the **Role**.
4. Confirm and sign the transaction.

On the mobile app, open the community's members screen. You can change the role of members whose current role is below yours.

## Community settings

Owners and admins see **Edit settings** on the community card. The **Community Settings** dialog has:

- **Title**: 3 to 20 characters, required.
- **About**: a short tagline, required.
- **Language**: required.
- **Description**: the longer text shown in the sidebar.
- **Rules**: one rule per line.
- **NSFW**: Yes or No. NSFW communities are marked on their card.
- **Default beneficiary**: a Hive username and a reward percentage (1 to 100). This one is stored on the community account itself, so you can only change it while you are logged in as the community account (the owner). Learn more in [Custom beneficiary](/hive/custom-beneficiary/).

The mobile app has the same settings screen (title, about, language, description, rules and "Not safe for work"), available to the owner and admins.

## Pin and unpin posts

Owners, admins and moderators can pin posts to the top of the community feed.

1. Open the post's menu (the three dots).
2. Choose **Pin to community**. To remove it later, choose **Unpin from community**.

## Mute and unmute posts

Muting hides a post from the community's normal feeds. Muted posts are still listed under the community's **Muted** tab.

1. Open the post's menu and choose **Mute**.
2. In the **Mute post/comment** dialog, add a note explaining your decision, then confirm.
3. To reverse it, choose **Unmute** and add a note.

Owners, admins and moderators can also mute comments from the discussion under a post.

## Mute a user

To stop an account from posting in your community, set their role to **muted** on the User Roles page. Their new posts are muted automatically. Set them back to **guest** to lift it.

## Members and activities

The community menu has two extra pages:

- **Members** (`ecency.com/subscribers/hive-xxxxxx`) lists everyone who joined.
- **Activities** (`ecency.com/activities/hive-xxxxxx`) shows the community account's recent activity feed, such as new subscribers and role changes.

## Community rewards

Ecency runs a community rewards program that pays **Ecency Points** to registered communities when their members publish posts with Ecency apps. See [Ecency Points](/ecency/ecency-points/).

To register:

1. Log in as the **community account itself** (the `hive-xxxxxx` account, using its own keys). Only the owner role sees the **Community Rewards** button on the community card. If you created the community on Ecency, your personal account is an admin, so it won't see this button.
2. Click **Community Rewards**, then **Register**.
3. Sign the transaction with your active key.

Requirements:

- The community needs at least **100 subscribers**. Below that you'll see "Minimum 100 subscribers required to register."
- Registration is one time. If the community is already registered, Ecency tells you so.
